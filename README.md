# Game Overview

What is Nsresurgence?

Nsresurgence is a browser-based game that challenges players to build their own fantasy world, managing resources, and competing with other players in real-time.

## How to Play

Controls:

- Use the mouse to click on elements (buildings, resources) on the map.
- Drag and drop items to move them around.
- Click on the "Economy" tab to manage your resources.
- Use the "Resource" menu to trade or sell items.

## 
Objective:

The goal is to be the first player to accumulate 1000 points by managing your resources, building structures, and competing with other players in real-time.

# Tips and Tricks for Success

Tips for Building a Strong Economy

- Start by gathering raw materials and crafting essential items.
- Build a variety of buildings to increase resource production.
- Use trade routes to connect different parts of your world and expand your resources.


Strategies for Competition

- Focus on building high-value structures that will attract players and increase competition.
- Keep an eye on other players' progress and adjust your strategy accordingly.
- Be strategic about when to attack or trade with other players.
